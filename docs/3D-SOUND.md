# The Door, full version: what you hear, and where

Headphones only. Directions are around your head: in front, to the right, behind, to the left, above, below.
Every position, level and timing is in `src/spatial-engine/cues.json` (from the spatial audio project, branch `ten-past-three`).
Add `?spatial=0` to the address to hear the original stereo version instead.

| Part | What you hear |
|---|---|
| Welcome and breathing | As before: the breathing drone and the voice, in front of you. Nothing moves. Part 1 was approved, so it is unchanged. |
| Witnessing, Movement 1 | The voice comes in close, dry and centred, as if right beside you. |
| Movement 3, the thought passing | On "Notice, it arrived from somewhere" the voice drifts to your left. Then it arcs slowly behind you, and "There it goes." is said as it passes behind, ending on your right. |
| Movement 4, stillness | The voice comes back to the centre, quieter and more open, with a little more room around it. |
| Movement 5, acceptance | A faint, airy layer starts as one narrow point in front and slowly spreads until it surrounds you. |
| Movement 6, transition | Everything thins to one low tone, which carries on into the threshold. |
| The threshold | On "Step through." the low tone opens into a whoosh, from a small point ahead out to all around you and slightly above. |
| The arrival | The new world fades in: wind from all around, some of it from above ("more sky"), and a stream low to your front right (a real water recording). |
| The trial | A low heartbeat-like pulse starts far ahead and comes closer through the whole trial. The wind picks up on "The wind picks up", and gets louder on "The wind is louder". |
| The door | On "The path opens." the pulse slows and moves away, and a warm rising tone comes up in front. |
| Raising your hands | A warm swell rises in front and spreads around you and above as your arms go up. |
| The effect (your word) | The sound of your word follows you. It spreads further around you the wider you open your arms, and it holds when you stop. It sits on the side of the screen where you stand. Kindness: chimes around you. Strength, courage, calm: their own layers, with the wind easing as your arms open. |
| Taking it in | Pinch: the sound around you draws in towards your fingertips. Fingertips to chest: it converges at your chest. "Let it in": it sinks in, darker and quieter, and a warm low tone blooms just below the chest. |
| The return | The effect and the world fade at the speed your hands come down. If your hands go back up, the sound comes back with them. Then the portal plays once more, softer and reversed, closing to a point in front. |
| Conclusion | The voice is close and dry again, back in the room. The last breath and the chime are as before. |

## Not yet heard by anyone

All of this was checked by measurement (which cue plays when, and where the voice is), not by listening. The things most likely to need adjusting are the levels of the wind and the trial's pulse under the voice, how far the voice moves in Movement 3, and the 20-second silence in Movement 6.
