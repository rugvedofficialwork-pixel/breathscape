# "The Door" — Full Session Script

**Total runtime: ~28 minutes** (stretch pauses/holds to reach 40, or tighten Stage 3 to land at 20)

**Voice direction throughout:** slow, unhurried, low-mid pitch, never performative. Let silences sit — the pauses marked `(...)` are not optional, they're where the work happens. Never rush toward the next line.

**Before starting:** the user is asked (on-screen, not spoken) to pick one word: **Kindness / Strength / Courage / Calm**. This choice steers Stage 3's ending. The core script below uses **Kindness** as the worked example — the other three variants are in the Appendix, same structure, swapped imagery only.

---

## PRE-SESSION (30 sec) — eyes open

> Welcome. Over the next half hour, nothing is being asked of you except this: breathe when I say breathe, listen when I say listen, and move when I say move. There's no right way to feel about any of it.
>
> (...)
>
> Get comfortable. When you're ready, we'll begin.

`[AUDIO: single warm tone, fades in and holds, centered]`
`[GESTURE: none yet — webcam frame appears, dim resting orb centered on screen]`

## STAGE 1 — GROUNDING: BOX BREATHING (~5 min) — eyes open

> Rest one hand out in front of you, palm up. We're going to breathe together, and your hand is going to breathe with you.

**Cycle 1**
> Breathe in — let your hand rise. (4s)
> (...) hold it there. (4s)
> Now let it go — hand comes back down. (4s)
> (...) and rest. (4s)

**Cycle 2**
> Again. In, and up. (4s)
> Hold. (4s)
> Out, and down. (4s)
> Rest. (4s)

**Cycle 3**
> In. (4s)
> Hold. (4s)
> Out. (4s)
> Rest. (4s)

**Cycle 4** *(voice softer, fewer words)*
> In... (4s)
> Hold... (4s)
> Out... (4s)
> Rest. (4s)

**Cycle 5** *(near-whisper)*
> In.
> Hold.
> Out.

**Cycle 6** *(silent except breath cue tones)*
> One more. However it comes.
> (...)
> Good.

> Let your hand rest completely. In a moment I'm going to ask you to close your eyes — you can do that now, whenever you're ready.

`[AUDIO: drone pitch rises on inhale, sustains on hold, falls on exhale; each cycle quieter and more desaturated in tone]`
`[GESTURE: hand height → orb radius, 1:1 mapping; orb desaturates warm→pale and slows ~10% each cycle; on final line, orb dims to a single point and screen fades toward dark]`

## STAGE 2 — WITNESSING: ACCEPTANCE (~7-8 min) — eyes closed, no gesture

*(Original narration — not a reproduction of any teacher's words. Camera can pause here; nothing is tracked.)*

**Movement 1 — Arriving**
> Eyes closed now. You don't have to relax on command — just notice what's actually here. The weight of your body. The sound of your own breath.
>
> (...)
>
> You don't need to change anything yet. Just arrive.

**Movement 2 — Noticing a thought**
> At some point in the next few minutes, a thought is going to show up. Maybe it already has.
>
> It might be about today. About someone you haven't resolved something with. About yourself.
>
> When it comes — don't chase it, and don't push it out. Just see that it's there. The way you'd notice a sound in another room. You don't have to go check on it.

**Movement 3 — The thought passing**
> Notice — it arrived from somewhere. (*voice begins to drift to one side*)
>
> And if you leave it alone, completely alone, it will go somewhere too. You don't have to help it leave. You don't have to hold the door for it either.
>
> (*voice arcs slowly around, as if passing behind the listener*)
>
> There it goes.
>
> (...)

**Movement 4 — Stillness**
> (*voice returns to center, quieter, more open/spacious*)
>
> Notice what's underneath, once you stop chasing the thought and stop fighting it.
>
> That underneath place — that's not empty. That's just you, without the argument.

**Movement 5 — Acceptance of what is**
> Whatever is actually true for you right now — the good, the unresolved, the tired, the fine — is allowed to be true. You don't have to fix it before you're allowed to rest in it.
>
> You are not behind. You are not late to your own life. You are exactly where you are.
>
> (...)

**Movement 6 — Transition**
> In a little while, there's somewhere I'm going to ask you to go. For now — just breathe. Nothing to solve.
>
> (...long pause...)
>
> When you hear the door, you'll know we're moving on. Stay exactly as you are until then.

`[AUDIO: voice starts dry/close-centered → drifts to one side (Movement 3) → arcs around the back via HRTF panning → returns to center with added reverb "openness" (Movement 4) → a second faint ambient layer widens around the listener (Movement 5) → fades toward silence, one low tone lingers into Stage 3's opening]`
`[GESTURE: none — screen can stay dark/off]`

## STAGE 3 — THE ADVENTURE: "THE DOOR" (~12-14 min)

### Part A — Threshold (eyes still closed)

> There's a door in front of you.
>
> It's already open.
>
> (...)
>
> Step through.

`[AUDIO: low whoosh sweeps front→fully surrounding — a portal enveloping the listener]`

### Part B — Arrival

> You're somewhere else now.
>
> Under your feet — soft ground, damp, cool. Somewhere close, water is moving. Above you, more sky than you're used to seeing at once.
>
> This place doesn't need you to understand it. It just needs you to walk.
>
> (...)
>
> So walk.

`[AUDIO: new ambient bed — wind, distant moving water, wide and spatial]`

### Part C — The Trial

> Ahead of you, the path narrows. The wind picks up — not dangerous, just insistent, like it's testing whether you'll turn back.
>
> There's a weight to carry here. Maybe it's literal — a stone, a bag, something heavy in your arms. Maybe it's just tiredness. Either way, it's yours to carry for this stretch.
>
> Keep walking. One step doesn't need to know about the next one.
>
> (...)
>
> It's harder now. The wind is louder. Your arms are tired.
>
> (...)
>
> And still — you're still walking.

`[AUDIO: low tension pulse builds, positioned in front, "approaching"; wind volume increases]`

### Part D — The Turn (the affirmation, woven in-scene)

> Here's what's true, even now, even tired, even carrying what you're carrying:
>
> You get to decide who you are in this exact moment. Not who you were an hour ago. Not who you'll have to be tomorrow. Just now.
>
> And right now — despite the wind, despite the weight — you choose kindness. Not because it's easy. Because you're choosing it anyway.
>
> The path opens.

`[AUDIO: tension pulse resolves — slows, then a clear rising tone replaces it, centered and steady]`

### Part E — Eyes Open, Gesture Trigger

> Open your eyes when you're ready.
>
> (...)
>
> Raise your hands. Let's see what happens.

`[AUDIO: rising harmonic swell, widening spatially as the gesture is detected]`
`[GESTURE TRIGGER: both wrists raised above shoulder height → starts effect layer]`

### Part F — The Effect (Kindness variant)

> Something's happening at your hands.
>
> Small points of light are gathering — soft, warm, the color of the last hour of daylight.
>
> The wider you open your arms, the more they spread. Watch — where they land, something grows. Small flowers, opening. Around your hands. Around you.
>
> This is what kindness looks like when it has somewhere to go. It doesn't erase what you carried to get here. It just grows around it, and through it.
>
> (...)
>
> Stay here a second. This is yours.

`[AUDIO: soft chime layer blooms outward from the user's on-screen position, HRTF-spread as effect intensifies]`
`[GESTURE INTENSITY: wrist-to-wrist distance → particle density/spread of flower-bloom effect; holds steady once user stops widening arms — does not auto-escalate]`

### Part G — Return

> Let your hands come down, slowly, whenever you're ready.
>
> (...)
>
> The flowers don't need you to hold your arms up to keep existing. They're already planted.
>
> The door is behind you now. You don't have to walk back through it to keep what you just felt.

`[AUDIO: effect sound fades in tandem with the world's ambient bed; portal whoosh plays once more, softer, reversed]`
`[GESTURE: hands lowering → effect fades at the same rate — an active choice, not an auto-timeout]`

---

## STAGE 4 — CONCLUSION: INTEGRATION (~3 min) — eyes open

> Whatever you noticed today — the thought you watched without chasing it, the thing you chose to carry it anyway and keep walking — you don't have to hold onto the feeling to keep what you learned from it.
>
> It's already yours. It doesn't expire when the session ends.
>
> (...)
>
> One more breath. In...
>
> (...)
>
> ...and out.
>
> That's it for today.

`[AUDIO: voice returns fully centered, dry — "back in the room"; single soft chime on the final exhale; ambient bed fades to silence over 5s]`
`[GESTURE: optional — one final hand-lower, mirroring Stage 1's close, for symmetry; screen visuals settle to a single steady glow, then fade to neutral]`

## APPENDIX — Word Variants for Stage 3, Parts D–F

Same structure, only the chosen word and its imagery change. Swap into Parts D and F.

### Acceptance (replaced Strength on 21 Nov 2026; see docs/WORDS-EVIDENCE.md)
**Part D:** "...you choose acceptance. Not giving up, and not pretending it's easy — just letting what's here be here, and walking on with it."
**Part F:** "The wind is still blowing, but it no longer pushes against you. It passes through your open hands, and on. The wider you open your arms, the more room there is — for the wind, for the weight, for whatever else came with you. None of it has to leave before you can stand here. This is what acceptance looks like. Not liking it. Not fighting it. Just making room."

### Courage
**Part D:** "...you choose courage. Not the absence of the wind — just deciding to walk into it anyway."
**Part F:** "The air around your hands starts to hold steady, even as the wind keeps moving past you — a small pocket of calm you're generating yourself. The wider you open your arms, the more ground around you goes quiet. This is what courage looks like: not a fearless place, just a held one, and you're the one holding it."

### Calm
**Part D:** "...you choose calm. Not because everything's resolved — because you're allowed to rest before it is."
**Part F:** "The wind around you slows first, then the water sound, then even your own heartbeat feels less urgent. The wider you open your arms, the further out the stillness spreads — like you're the center of it, not the target of it. This is what calm looks like when it isn't forced. It just spreads, because you stopped fighting it."

## ADDED: Part G variants (written for this prototype, not in the original script)

Part G line 1 is unchanged. Lines 2 and 3 now vary by word: the flowers line is Kindness-only, and the "door behind you" line was replaced by a "walk out and it stays with you" line.

**Kindness** — 2: "The flowers don't need you to hold your arms up to keep existing. They're already planted." 3: "When you walk out of here and back into your day, the kindness goes with you. It doesn't need anything from you to stay."
**Acceptance** — 2: "You don't have to keep your arms open for the room to stay. It's already made, and it's already yours." 3: "When you walk out of here and back into your day, this room goes with you. Whatever arrives, there will be space for it."
**Courage** — 2: "You don't have to keep your arms open to hold the calm you made. It's already yours, and it moves with you." 3: "When you walk out of here and back into your day, this held place goes with you. You can find it again whenever you need it."
**Calm** — 2: "The stillness doesn't need your arms held wide to stay. It has already settled, and it's already yours." 3: "When you walk out of here and back into your day, the stillness goes with you. It will still be there when you need it."
